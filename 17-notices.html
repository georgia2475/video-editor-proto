AAC encoding fallback

@mediabunny/aac-encoder is used when native AAC encoding is unavailable. Its wrapper is MPL 2.0. Its WebAssembly component contains unmodified FFmpeg under LGPL 2.1 or later. No GPL or nonfree components are enabled in that upstream build.

The included package README identifies the exact FFmpeg revision, Emscripten version, build flags and steps to rebuild or replace the encoder. Corresponding FFmpeg source revision. Complete corresponding FFmpeg source download. AAC extension project.
